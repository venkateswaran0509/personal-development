The 12-week roadmap

Move up when the current level feels controlled—not just because the calendar says so.

WEEKS 01–04

01 · Adapt

Learn form, make training a habit, and finish every set with energy left. Begin with 1–2 sets.

WEEKS 05–08

02 · Strengthen

Build toward 2–3 sets. Increase reps first; make a movement harder only when form stays solid.

WEEKS 09–12

03 · Progress

Work toward 3 controlled sets and harder variations. Add a safe pulling option if possible.
